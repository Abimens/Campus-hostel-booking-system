# Campus Hostel Booking and Management System

## 1. Background
Students often face difficulties in finding hostel accommodation around campus. They may have to visit different hostels physically to find available rooms, compare prices and obtain information about facilities. This process can be time-consuming and inconvenient.

The Campus Hostel Booking and Management System will provide an online platform where students can view hostels, check room availability and submit booking requests.

## 2. Problem Statement
Finding hostel accommodation around campus can be difficult due to limited information about available rooms, prices, locations and facilities. Students often have to visit hostels physically before knowing whether rooms are available. This creates inconvenience and wastes time. Therefore, there is a need for a system that makes hostel information accessible and allows students to submit booking requests online.

## 3. Aim
To develop a Campus Hostel Booking and Management System that enables students to search for hostels, check room availability and submit booking requests online.

## 4. Objectives
- Allow students to register and log in.
- Display hostels, prices, locations and facilities.
- Allow students to check room availability.
- Enable students to submit booking requests.
- Allow hostel managers to manage rooms and bookings.
- Send booking status notifications.

## 5. Core Features
1. Student Registration and Login.
2. Hostel Search and Listings.
3. Room Availability.
4. Room Booking and Management.
5. Email Notifications.

## 6. Target Users
- Students
- Hostel Managers
- Administrators
